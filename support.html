Briefly support

Briefly rewrites PDF, Word, text and Markdown documents at the length and reading level you choose, using Apple Intelligence on your Mac. If something isn't working, the answers below cover the usual causes. If not, email andrew.parnell1@ucd.ie and I'll do my best to help.

Briefly says Apple Intelligence isn't available

Briefly needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. After you turn it on, macOS downloads the model in the background, which can take a while. Briefly will start working once that finishes.

"This PDF has no selectable text"

The PDF is probably a scan, which is a picture of the pages rather than text. Briefly can only shorten documents that contain text. Running the scan through a text recognition (OCR) tool first will usually fix this.

Some lengths are missing

Briefly only offers lengths that are clearly shorter than the original, so a short document has fewer choices. For very long documents, Briefly summarises the first 100 pages.

The first version takes a while

The first version usually takes 10 to 20 seconds. Briefly then prepares the other lengths and reading levels in the background, so moving a slider is usually instant. You can change this in Briefly › Settings › General.

A version got something wrong

Like any AI summary, a version can occasionally get a detail wrong. The Original / Briefly switch at the top of the window takes you straight back to the source, so you can check.

Word documents look a little different

Briefly shows Word documents as plain pages: headings, text, lists and fonts come through, but pictures are left out and tables are shown one row per line.

Privacy

Briefly doesn't connect to the internet and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
